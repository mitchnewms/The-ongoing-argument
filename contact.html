What this is, and what it is not

The Ongoing Argument is a self guided, AI assisted communication tool. It is not therapy, counseling, medical care, or legal advice, and it is not a crisis service.

You work through written steps on your own, at your own pace. An AI helps you put words to what you notice, and you stay in charge of what you write and what you share with your partner. It does not diagnose anyone and it does not replace a licensed professional.

If you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number. In the United States you can call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, or call the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233. This tool is not designed for relationships where there is violence, threats or fear. If that is your situation, please talk to a qualified person first.
